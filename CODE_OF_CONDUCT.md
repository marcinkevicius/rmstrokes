# Code of Conduct

Be kind and assume good intent. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't OK, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else connected
to the project. Neither is publishing someone's private information.

If something feels wrong, report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability";
start the title with "Conduct:"). The maintainer will look into it and may edit or remove comments, or block people
who keep crossing the line.

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
and its spirit applies.
